As an official REA partner, we can access your listings directly. To authorise this connection, please follow these steps:

  • Log into your Agent Admin account
  • Click XML Uploads from the left-hand menu
  • Click the Change my uploader button
  • Select the relevant API from the What will this uploader be used for? drop-down list
  • Select "Voxa Realty" from the Who is your new provider? drop-down list
  • Complete and submit the form

Please note: If you are unable to find "Voxa Realty" in the drop-down list, our name might not have populated yet. In this instance, select the first option in the drop-down list and manually type "Voxa Realty" in the More Info section.

Please let me know if you encounter any issues during this process.

Once authorised, we will need a couple of days to finalise the integration and conduct thorough testing. As soon as we have confirmed everything is operating smoothly, I will send you the final setup instructions and the link to configure your subscription payment.

After your setup is complete, we will run some final system tests, and you will be ready to go!
